  • Language

Failed to connect to the scanner.

Cause

The issue may be caused by the following:

  • The scanner is not turned on

  • The scanner is not connected to the computer with the USB cable

  • The scanner is in the middle of the start-up process

  • The scanner settings are being changed

  • Multiple scanners are connected to the computer

  • Another application is using the scanner

  • The scanner that is connected to the computer is not supported by Certificate Registration Tool

Solution

Perform the following:

  • Turn on the scanner.

  • Connect the scanner to the computer with the USB cable that is supplied with the scanner.

  • Check that the scanner is not in the middle of the start-up process.

  • Finish changing the scanner settings.

  • Connect only one scanner to the computer.

  • Exit the other application that is using the scanner.

  • Check which scanner models are supported by Certificate Registration Tool.

    For details, refer to Supported Scanner Models.

If a message is still displayed after taking measures above, perform the following:

  1. Turn off the scanner and turn it on again.

    TIP

    When you try to turn off the scanner, the message "Operating..." may keep appearing on the touch screen of the scanner. Even if this occurs, turning off the scanner should not be a problem.

  2. Restart the computer.

  3. Restart Certificate Registration Tool.
